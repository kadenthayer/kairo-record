    How a record earns trust

    What it tells you

    What the evidence shows, and how sure we are. Each claim carries a confidence rating and the observations behind it. Where something can't be determined, the record says so.

    A Kairo Supply record documents identity and condition. It is not a valuation; for insurance or tax, a credentialed appraiser takes it from here.

    How the seal works

    Every file is fingerprinted with SHA-256 when it arrives. Each ledger entry includes the fingerprint of the one before it, so changing any past entry breaks every entry after it.

    Anyone can check. The record page re-hashes the ledger and the published images in your browser. Anchoring the seal to a public blockchain is next.
