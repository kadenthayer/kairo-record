The Kairo standard

Five steps, in this order, for every object. A record shows which are complete.

  1. Micro-imaging

    Close-ups of every diagnostic surface, focus-stacked, glare controlled, scale calibrated.

  2. UV screening

    Filtered 365 nm light. Glue, filler and paint fluoresce differently from the original material.

  3. Measurement

    Dimensions to 0.01 mm and weight to 0.01 g, tying the record to the physical object.

  4. Overall views

    Six orientations with a forensic scale and a color reference.

  5. Condition report

    Findings, confidence and care notes, sealed into the ledger.

What a record tells you

What the evidence shows, and how sure we are. Each claim carries a confidence rating and the observations behind it. Where something can't be determined from the evidence, the record says so.

A Kairo record documents identity and condition. It is not a valuation. For insurance or tax purposes, take it to a credentialed appraiser, who will find it a useful starting point.

How the seal works

Every file is fingerprinted with SHA-256 when it arrives. Each ledger entry includes the fingerprint of the entry before it, so changing any past entry breaks every one after it.

Anyone can check this. The record page re-hashes the ledger and the published images in your browser. Anchoring the seal to a public blockchain is the next step.
